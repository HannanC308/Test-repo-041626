# Project: Pocket Pet Caregiver

A gamified chore tracker where checking off real-world pet duties keeps a virtual pet happy and healthy.

## 1. Features
*   **Pet Status Bars:** Three health meters: Hunger, Cleanliness, and Happiness (0-100%).
*   **Task Triggers:** Checking "Feed the Dog" increases the Hunger meter by 30%.
*   **Pet Animation/Image:** Changes from 😢 to 😃 based on average status scores.
