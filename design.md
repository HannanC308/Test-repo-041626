# Project: 5th Grade Trivia Blitz

A fun, multiple-choice quiz game covering elementary history, science, and math.

## 1. Features
*   **Question Display:** Shows one large question at a time with 4 choice buttons.
*   **Instant Feedback:** Turns green for correct answers, red for incorrect answers.
*   **Score Counter:** Keeps track of points (e.g., "+10 points" per correct answer).
*   **Timer Countdown:** A simple 15-second visual countdown per question.
